Frequently Asked Questions

Do subscription plans auto-renew?
Yes. All subscription plans auto-renew by default. You can manage your subscription anytime. Credits do not auto-renew.
When does daily quota reset?
The daily quota resets at 00:00 UTC. Any unused quota is not carried forward to the next day.
What is the usage priority between Credits and subscription quota?
Purchased Credits are used first. Once they are exhausted, your subscription's daily quota is used.
How do upgrades work?
You can subscribe to a higher plan and the difference is prorated. Learn more →
Why is the Word export feature paid?
Most DeepShare features are free. Word export requires server resources for formatting and processing, and a small fee helps keep the service stable.
Does the API Key expire?
Yes, the API Key is valid for 1 year from the purchase date. Any purchase made within its validity period automatically extends its validity by another year.
What if I forget my API Key?
Contact us with the email address you used for purchase and we will help recover it.
How long until credits are added after payment?
Credits are added immediately after successful payment, and your API Key is sent by email.
Other questions?
Please send an email to: contact@deepshare.app
